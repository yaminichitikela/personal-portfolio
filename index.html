Yamini Chitikela

PhD Student at Old Dominion University, Montclair State MS CS '23

Hi! I broadly work on learning from graphs and hypergraphs, and on using them for problems in biology and medicine. I am currently a 1st year PhD student at Old Dominion University advised by Dr. Chunjiang Zhu. Before that, I did my MS in CS at Montclair State University and my B.Tech at JNTU Kakinada.

To understand what I'm thinking about right now, read in order:

  • Generalization of hypergraph neural networks.
  • Graph-based QSAR for PubChem assays and human liver toxicity.
  • BioLit AutoMiner, a multi-agent system for mining biomedical literature.

Some other notable things worth mentioning:

  • I spoke at the Women in Tech Global Conference 2025.
  • I was a Grand Award Judge at Regeneron ISEF 2026. I've also judged Technovation Girls, Sigma Xi IFoRE '26, the Tidewater Science Fair, and the ocMaker Challenge.
  • I review for IEEE ICASSP 2027, IEEE BHI, and NCUR 2026.
  • I am the President of the Computer Science Graduate Society at ODU, where we run Trick or Research and wrote a Grad Survival Guide.
  • I mentor undergrads at the GraLNA NSF REU site at ODU, and I taught as an adjunct at NJIT in 2023.

I spent a few years in industry at ERPHealth and Tata Consultancy Services before starting my PhD. Feel free to reach out to talk about anything through my email at [x]@odu.edu where [x]=ychit001.
